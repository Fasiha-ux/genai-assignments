**NEXUS TECHNOLOGIES CORP. – INTERNAL EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & POLICIES (2026) – Q&A GUIDE**

---

## 1. What are the standard working hours for all employees?
**Answer:** The standard working hours are **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST**, Monday through Friday.

* Applies to every employee unless otherwise specified.
* The schedule defines the core business day for the company.

---

## 2. When are “Core Collaboration Hours,” and what is expected of staff during this time?
**Answer:** Core Collaboration Hours run from **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**.

* All staff must be reachable on **Slack** throughout this window.
* This period is intended for real‑time communication and teamwork.

---

## 3. How does the Hybrid Work (remote) policy work?
**Answer:** Employees may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**.

1. Request prior approval from the **direct manager**.
2. Ensure availability during Core Collaboration Hours on Slack.
3. Remote work is limited to the approved number of days each week.

---

## 4. Is there any financial support for setting up a home office, and who is eligible?
**Answer:** Yes. A **one‑time stipend of $500** is provided **to full‑time employees** upon hiring for home‑office equipment.

* The stipend is only for the initial setup.
* It is not available to part‑time or contractor staff.

---

## 5. How many vacation days do full‑time employees receive each calendar year?
**Answer:** Full‑time employees receive **18 days of paid vacation leave** per calendar year.

* Days are counted as calendar days (including weekends if taken).
* Unused days may be rolled over according to the rollover policy (see Q6).

---

## 6. What is the vacation rollover rule, and when do rolled‑over days expire?
**Answer:** A maximum of **5 unused vacation days** can be carried over to the next calendar year.

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Maximum rollover | 5 days |
| Expiration date | **March 31** of the following year |
| Eligibility | Only for full‑time employees who have unused vacation days at year‑end |

---

## 7. What sick‑leave benefits are provided, and what documentation is required for extended absences?
**Answer:** Employees receive **10 paid sick days** annually.

* If sick leave exceeds **3 consecutive business days**, a **medical note** must be submitted.

---

## 8. What parental‑leave options exist for primary and secondary caregivers?
**Answer:**

| Caregiver Type | Paid Leave Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Primary caregiver | **16 weeks** (fully paid) |
| Secondary caregiver | **6 weeks** (fully paid) |

* Both categories receive full pay for the stated period.

---

## 9. Which employees receive a laptop, and what model is provided?
**Answer:** **All software engineers** receive a **16‑inch MacBook Pro (M‑series)**.

* The laptop is supplied by the company as standard equipment.

---

## 10. Who is eligible for the monthly phone allowance, and how much is reimbursed?
**Answer:** The phone allowance applies to **senior managers and client‑facing roles**.

* Reimbursement amount: **$75 per month** for mobile phone expenses.

---

## 11. What learning & development (L&D) stipend is available, and what is the approval process?
**Answer:** Employees receive an **annual stipend of $1,200** for courses, books, or technical certifications.

1. Identify the desired L&D activity.
2. Submit a **pre‑approval request via the HR portal**.
3. Upon approval, expenses are reimbursed up to the $1,200 limit.

---

## 12. What is the daily meal allowance for business travel, and how is it applied?
**Answer:** The **travel per diem** provides a **$65 daily meal allowance** for employees traveling for business.

* The amount is paid per travel day, regardless of actual meal costs, unless otherwise specified by policy.

---

## 13. What are the flight‑class booking rules for domestic and international travel?
**Answer:**

| Flight Type | Maximum Duration | Allowed Class |
|-------------|------------------|---------------|
| Domestic | < 6 hours | **Economy** |
| International | > 8 hours | **Business** |

* Flights that do not meet the duration thresholds must follow the listed class.

---

## 14. How must expense receipts be submitted, and what is the deadline?
**Answer:** All receipts **over $25** must be submitted **via Concur** within **14 days of travel completion**.

* Receipts ≤ $25 are not required to be submitted.
* The 14‑day window is strict; late submissions may be denied.

---

## 15. Summarize the key steps an employee should follow when planning a business trip.
**Answer:**

1. **Book travel** respecting flight‑class rules (economy for domestic < 6 h, business for international > 8 h).
2. **Track daily meals** to stay within the $65 per‑diem allowance.
3. **Collect all receipts** over $25 during the trip.
4. **Submit expense report** through Concur **within 14 days** after returning.

---

## 16. What are the overall expectations for remote‑work availability and communication?
**Answer:**

* Employees must be reachable on **Slack** during **Core Collaboration Hours (10 AM–3 PM EST)**.
* Remote work is limited to **2 days per week** with prior manager approval.
* The $500 remote‑work stipend is a one‑time benefit for full‑time hires.
