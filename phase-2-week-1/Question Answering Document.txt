===================================================================
                  NEXUS TECHNOLOGIES CORP.
            INTERNAL EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & POLICIES (2026)
===================================================================

1. WORKING HOURS & REMOTE WORK POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday.
- Core Collaboration Hours are between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST. All staff 
  must be reachable on Slack during core hours.
- Hybrid Work: Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week 
  with prior approval from their direct manager.
- Remote work stipend: A one-time stipend of $500 is provided to full-time 
  employees upon hiring for home office equipment setup.

2. PAID TIME OFF (PTO) & LEAVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Vacation Leave: Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick days annually. A medical note 
  is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive business days.
- Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave; 
  secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Unused Vacation Rollover: A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may be carried 
  over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31st.

3. HARDWARE & EQUIPMENT EXPENSES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Laptops: All software engineers receive a 16-inch MacBook Pro (M-series).
- Phone Allowance: Senior managers and client-facing roles receive a monthly 
  mobile phone reimbursement of $75.
- Learning & Development: Employees receive an annual stipend of $1,200 for 
  courses, books, or technical certifications. Submissions require pre-approval 
  via the HR portal.

4. TRAVEL & MEAL REIMBURSEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Travel Per Diem: Employees traveling for business receive a $65 daily meal allowance.
- Flight Class: Economy class must be booked for domestic flights under 6 hours. 
  Business class is permitted for international flights over 8 hours.
- Expense Reports: All receipts over $25 must be submitted via Concur within 
  14 days of travel completion.
